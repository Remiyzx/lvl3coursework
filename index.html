📋 Quick Script Rules

  • Structure: Include 3–4 scenes total.
  • Length: Write at least 25 valid lines per student (3 or more Pinyin syllables per line).
  • Scope: Use Kuaile Xue Hanyu and Textbook Lessons 1–11 only (maximum 4 highlighted external words allowed).

🎬 Suggested Storyline Outline

🚀 Creative Plot Twist: Everyone pretends to forget the birthday person's special day because of their busy daily routines, then surprises them with a cake and gifts!
SCENE 01

Planning the Party & Checking Schedules

  • Friends talk about today's date and realize a friend's birthday is coming soon (Lesson 9).
  • Decide to organize a fun birthday party (shēngri jùhuì).
  • Compare daily routines—what time everyone wakes up, goes to class, and finishes class—to set the time for shopping and the party (Lesson 11).
SCENE 02

Shopping for Party Supplies & Gifts

  • Go to the mall or shop to prepare for the party.
  • Discuss what to buy, such as a cake (dàngāo), one jīn of fruit, CDs (guāngpán), or notebooks (běnzi).
  • Ask the seller for prices (duōshao qián), talk about whether items are expensive (guì), and pay (Lesson 10).
SCENE 03

At the Birthday Party

  • Welcome everyone to the party, including a new friend (briefly ask which country they are from and what they study to cover Lessons 7 & 8).
  • Ask about the birthday person's birth date, exact birthday, and how old they are turning (Lesson 9).
  • Present the gifts, sing, and talk about what food and drinks everyone is enjoying at the party (Lesson 9).
SCENE 04

Wrapping Up a Fun Night

  • Check the time—it is getting late, and friends discuss what time they need to go home and sleep for tomorrow's routine (Lesson 11).
  • End the scene by saying the birthday party was really fun (Shēngri jùhuì zhēn yǒu yìsi!), tying back to the main topic.
